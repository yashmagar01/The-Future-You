Privacy Policy

The Future You — AI Personal Communication Coach

Effective date: 24 November 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to The Future You. We provide an AI-powered self-reflection and communication tool where users upload short videos to receive automated feedback on clarity, tone, posture, and speaking style.

This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, where we store it, how long we keep it, and your rights. We aim for clear, simple language: we only collect what we need, and we never sell your data.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Account information

We use Supabase Auth for authentication. We store basic account data such as email address and hashed password (Supabase handles password encryption). We also record device and login metadata (IP, browser, OS) for security and debugging.

2.2 Video & audio data

When you upload a 5–10 minute video, the raw video file is stored in Supabase Storage. We extract audio locally using FFmpeg for analysis. No human reviews your raw videos unless you explicitly opt into a research program.

2.3 AI-generated analysis

We analyze videos using local, open-source models running on our compute layer. Typical models in use at MVP:

  • Vosk — offline speech-to-text (transcripts & timestamps)
  • Transformers.js — clarity, filler-word detection, simple summarization
  • MediaPipe Pose — posture and gesture detection

The outputs (transcript, clarity score, tone summary, posture markers, and structured insights) are stored as JSON in the Supabase database.

2.4 Internal logs and analytics

We store internal logs and usage counters (upload success, feature usage, streaks) inside Supabase for product reliability and debugging. We do not use external analytics providers like Google Analytics in this MVP.

3. How we use your data

We use your data to provide and improve the service:

  • Process videos and produce insights shown on your dashboard.
  • Maintain authentication and account security.
  • Improve product reliability and offline model accuracy (beta users only).

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or send your raw videos to third-party AI services. All core analysis happens using local/open-source tools and Supabase.

4. Storage and retention

Normal users

Videos are deleted within 24–48 hours after automated processing completes.

Beta users

If you sign up as a beta tester and consent, we may store videos for up to 12 months to improve our models. You may withdraw consent any time and we will delete your stored videos within 7 days.

Transcripts and analysis

Transcripts and analysis results are retained in Supabase until you request deletion or you delete your account. Temporary processing files are removed after analysis.

5. Security

We follow secure practices appropriate for this MVP:

  • HTTPS/TLS for all traffic.
  • Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) to ensure users only access their own data.
  • Signed upload URLs and limited access to storage objects.
  • Temporary files are removed after processing; we do not keep video data in logs.

6. Data sharing

We do not share your personal data with third-party AI providers, advertisers, or data brokers. The only external service we use is Supabase for hosting storage, database, and authentication.

7. Your rights

You control your data. You can request:

  • Account deletion (removes videos, transcripts, and insights)
  • Data export (download transcripts and analysis results)
  • Withdrawal of beta consent (stop any future research use)

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@thefutureyou.app or use the account settings page.

8. Cookies

We only use essential cookies via Supabase Auth for login sessions. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies in the MVP.

9. Children

The service is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 created an account, we will delete their data promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our tech stack or data practices change (for example, if we start using paid cloud APIs). Significant changes will be presented to users via email or an in-app banner.

11. Contact

For privacy requests or questions: privacy@thefutureyou.app.

Short summary: We use local open-source models and Supabase. We do not sell your data.
